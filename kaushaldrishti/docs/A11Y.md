## 1. Executive Summary

KaushalDrishti is designed strictly in accordance with **WCAG 2.2 Level AA** standards and the **Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0)**. Because District Skill Development Officers (DSDO) and planners frequently operate in low-connectivity areas or use specialized assistive technologies, accessibility is built into the core design rather than retrofitted.

---

## 2. Implemented Accessibility Features

### 2.1 Visual & Color Independence
- **No Information by Color Alone**: Every status (Acute Shortage, Emerging Shortage, Approaching Saturation, Saturated, Balanced) is encoded by **color**, **explicit text badge**, **mathematical probability ($p_S / p_O$)**, and **severity score**.
- **Prometheus Brand Palette Contrast**:
  - Deep Navy: `#021861` (Backgrounds, primary header surfaces)
  - Secondary Electric Blue: `#255DCE` (Buttons, active tabs, interactive borders)
  - Accent Crimson: `#DE1110` (Acute Shortage, priority alerts, golden path indicator)
  - Contrast ratios against white/slate backgrounds exceed **7:1** (AAA standard for text); on dark navy cards, text is `#ffffff` or `#f8fafc`, exceeding **11:1** contrast.

### 2.2 Low-Bandwidth & Accessible Alternative Mode
- Dedicated **Low-Bandwidth Mode** toggle present in the navigation bar.
- When toggled, all SVG interactive charts and heavy animations are replaced with **semantic, screen-reader-optimized HTML tables** with exact numerical figures, monthly forecast intervals, and ARIA labels.
- Reduces page payload and eliminates GPU rendering overhead for legacy district computers.

### 2.3 Keyboard Navigation & Semantics
- **Full Keyboard Operability**: All navigation tabs, trade switchers, policy levers, and modals are navigable via standard `Tab`, `Shift+Tab`, `Space`, and `Enter`.
- **Semantic Landmarks**: Strict usage of HTML5 semantic tags: `<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`, `<button>`, `<table>`, `<thead>`, `<tbody>`.
- **Form Controls & Unique IDs**: All slider controls in the Policy Scenario Lab utilize dynamic unique React IDs (`useId()`) with associated `<label htmlFor="...">`.

### 2.4 Multilingual & Screen Reader Support
- **Multilingual Support**: English (`en`), Hindi (`hi`), Kannada (`kn`), and Tamil (`ta`).
- Language switcher preserves state across all navigation screens and announces active language changes.
- Numbers follow standardized decimal conventions; descriptions in regional languages are deterministic and audited for screen-reader pronunciation.

### 2.5 Printable Executive District Brief
- Standard `@media print` CSS rules automatically strip interactive navigation, dark backgrounds, and controls.
- Generates a high-contrast black-and-white 1-page executive brief formatted for official District Skill Committee (DSC) physical binders and offline review.
